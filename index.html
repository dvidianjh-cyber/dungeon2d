    MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

    A little field guide.

    Walk with WASD / arrow keys or the touch arrows. Click an object, or press E beside one. The action panel lets you inspect, talk, take and enter.

    Choose an item in your inventory to use or drop it. To give Adelle her key, select Adelle, then choose the key and press Use on Adelle. For a film, use a ticket on the projector.

    At a shop, click Buy to browse stock or select a carried item and Sell. Shops buy for half the listed price. The bank holds your gold during the session.

    Host friends creates a room code. Friends choose Join friends; the host shares the world automatically. Up to eight players can explore different interiors in the same room.

    Import or download a .dungeon file from the menu. It is a ZIP containing JSON metadata and all linked packs. Sessions start fresh; imported packs and preferences stay in this browser.

    Online rooms use PeerJS signalling and WebRTC. Some restrictive networks require a TURN relay. Keep the host’s tab open while playing.

    Call it a day?

    This session’s progress will end. Your installed dungeon packs will stay here.
